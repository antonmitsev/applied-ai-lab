## Important

You are interacting with an AI assistant, not a person. It can be wrong and
does not replace a qualified professional, an on-site inspection, manufacturer
instructions, or an emergency service.

If there is immediate danger to life, fire, or electricity, stop, move away if
safe, and call 112. Do not touch water or equipment near electricity. Do not
work on gas, combustion, or electrical systems based on this assistant.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

The assistant can explain visible components, ask focused questions, suggest
limited low-risk checks, and cite sources. It cannot remotely confirm pressure,
temperature, hidden pipework, material, size/thread, electrical safety,
structural condition, or exact compatibility from a description or image alone.

Scope includes domestic water supply, drainage, and the water side of heating
systems—risers, valves, radiators, vents, heat meters, manifolds, and hydronic
underfloor heating. Gas, combustion, electrical repair, and work requiring
professional authorisation are out of scope.

## When to stop

Stop and seek qualified help for an uncontrolled active leak, water near
electricity, very hot water/steam, an unknown source or isolation valve, risk to
a shared building system, suspected gas/combustion, visible damage to a
pressure vessel, or when the answer conflicts with the real situation.

Do not use force, dismantle a pressurised component, or rely on a guess about
exact size or compatibility. Exact manufacturer instructions and a safe on-site
assessment take priority.

## Response levels

- **Critical:** normal steps stop; only pre-reviewed immediate risk-limiting
  actions are displayed.
- **Caution:** only limited safe checks and professional handoff are allowed; no
  generated repair steps are shown.
- **Routine:** steps are allowed only with sufficient information, no material
  unknown, and adequate sources.

If material information is missing, the assistant must ask or stop rather than
guess. Image evidence cannot lower an already established risk.

## Reports and corrections

Report unsafe, inaccurate, or outdated output through the corrections channel
that will be published before public release. Do not resend personal or
sensitive information. Where possible, provide the language, approximate date,
claim, and a reliable public source.
