## How sources are selected

The assistant prefers a reviewed library of official instructions for the exact
manufacturer/product, applicable public authorities, and technical standards or
guidance with clear provenance. Reputable secondary material may support
context and discovery but is not the sole basis for safety or exact
compatibility.

Live web search is used only when the reviewed library is insufficient or
current information is required. A search result does not automatically become
a trusted source. The system must check URL, domain, applicability, date,
status, and the relationship between the source and the specific claim.

## Citations and limits

Verifiable technical, safety, and compatibility claims must have visible cited
support. Titles and URLs come from a controlled registry, not unconstrained
model output. If evidence is insufficient, the assistant must say so, ask a
focused question, or hand off to a professional.

A citation does not mean that the author, manufacturer, or authority endorses
the project. The project does not copy or republish third-party documentation
without permission; it stores content only where rights or authorisation are
recorded. External pages may change or disappear.

## Manufacturers and professionals

Research into manufacturers and their instructions is a separate, unfinished
WP-01 work package. A future public-professional directory is a separate WP-02
package. If added, it must be neutral, have no paid placement, advertising, or
quality guarantee, and publish its inclusion and correction criteria.

## Review and corrections

Exact-compatibility and safety sources are reviewed at least every 90 days and
may be withdrawn immediately for safety, accuracy, change, or rights concerns.
Send corrections through the contact channel that will be published before
public release, identifying the URL, affected claim, and reason. Do not send a
protected document copy unless you are entitled to share it.

Also see the [Safety Notice](safety.en.md).
