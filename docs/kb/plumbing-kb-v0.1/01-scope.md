# Domestic Plumbing KB Scope

## 1. Primary goal

The knowledge base must support:

- identifying components and connections;
- explaining how they work;
- determining what actually seals a connection;
- symptom-based diagnosis;
- choosing a safe diagnostic test;
- preparing for a repair;
- selecting a suitable consumable or seal;
- recognizing when a DIY repair must stop.

## 2. Audience

The primary user is a technically capable home improver who:

- can use basic hand tools;
- can measure with calipers;
- often has a photograph but does not know the exact term;
- wants to understand the cause, not only receive a “tighten it” instruction;
- prefers diagnosis over replacing parts blindly.

## 4. Out of scope for v1

The following topics are deferred:

- drainage and wastewater systems;
- toilet cisterns and sanitary fixtures;
- pumps and pressure-boosting systems;
- underfloor heating;
- boilers and combustion systems;
- gas installations;
- industrial plumbing;
- whole-system design;
- regulatory calculations.

## 5. Safety boundary

The KB may explain principles and diagnostics, but must clearly mark elevated
risk for:

- work on a water heater connected to mains electricity;
- work on a pressurized system;
- hot water and scalding risk;
- shared risers;
- central heating;
- work where a mistake could cause flooding;
- suspected structural damage to a pipe or fitting.

Gas installations are not part of the DIY content.

## 6. Non-functional requirements

The documentation must be:

- RAG-friendly;
- readable by people;
- vendor-neutral;
- independent of a specific database;
- extensible;
- versionable;
- suitable for Git;
- primarily Markdown plus YAML frontmatter;
- free of critical information that exists only in images.
